Week 2 Headline

Eagles split Week 2 at 3–3

50% Series Wins  ·  2 Sweeps

Parents, Players & Fans,

Welcome to the 1st Edition of The Esports Report! We're excited to bring you news from inside the Hartland Esports Program.

This week was our first week of the Fall 2026 Regular Season. There were lots of highlights, a fair amount of technical kinks to work out, and lots of fun memories created. Your students definitely rocked it this week and made the week go a lot smoother than it probably should have. We thank you and your students for your patience as we continue to navigate our league switching platforms and all of the challenges that have come with that.

Overall, our teams had a great week and solid start to the season. We look forward to seeing how our players will grow from Week 1 to Week 8!

Go Eagles!
